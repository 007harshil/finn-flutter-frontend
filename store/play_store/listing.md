# FiNN — Play Store listing text

**App name (30):** FiNN: Budget & Expense Tracker

**Short description (78/80):** Track spending, budgets, goals and EMIs. Tell the AI assistant, approve, done.

**Full description:**

FiNN is a calm, private money tracker for Indian households. Record what you spend and earn, plan budgets, save towards goals and keep an eye on EMIs, subscriptions and money friends owe you, all in one place.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Track income and expenses across cash, bank accounts and cards
• Set monthly budgets per category and get a warning before you overspend
• Save towards goals and build an emergency fund
• Track loans and EMIs you pay, and money you lent
• Never miss a renewal: subscriptions and upcoming dues in one list
• Split shared expenses and see who owes whom
• Add friends by their FiNN ID and share pending items with them
• Insights: where your money went, month by month
• Export your data as CSV, JSON or a PDF report

AI ASSISTANT
Just say or type what happened, like "Paid 450 for lunch in cash" or "Put 5,000 into my Goa trip goal". The assistant prepares the change and you approve every change before it is saved. Replies are generated by AI and can be wrong; you can report any reply from inside the app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No bank logins and no SMS reading: you decide what to record
• No ads, no tracking SDKs, and we never sell your data
• Your records are saved securely to your FiNN account, so you can sign in on a new phone
• Delete your account and all its data anytime from inside the app

PLEASE NOTE
FiNN is a personal record-keeping and budgeting tool. It does not offer loans, credit, payments, banking, investments or insurance, and it never moves money. A free FiNN account (email sign-in) is required.
