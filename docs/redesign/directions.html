Three ways FiNN could look and move

Each direction is built from the research (FiNN Design Research) and FiNN's own data: Aarav's accounts, October budgets and real transactions from the demo seed. All three keep every existing feature and number; they change presentation, layout and motion.

Use Light / Dark on each direction, and Play motion to see its signature moments. On a phone, swipe the mockups sideways.

FiNN today

Captured from the running app on the demo seed, light theme. Solid information, a utility look: one green, ink everywhere, a nav bar that collides with the add button, and a typeface whose zero reads as Ø in money.

Current

Calm precision, drawn from the two flowing arrows in FiNN's logo. Money is shown as a current that comes in and divides. Quiet surfaces, one deep teal, nothing shouts.

Type

₹72,932.00

Schibsted Grotesk for everything: 650 for figures with tabular digits, 700 for titles, 400 for text. A newsroom grotesk that keeps long Indian numbers tight and has a plain zero.

Buttons and cards

Buttons 50 px, 14 px radius, deep teal. Cards are flat: 16 px radius, hairline border, no shadow; only the hero gets the deep slab (22 px).

Motion personality: fluid, never bouncy

  • Data: ease-out (0.2, 0, 0, 1), 320 ms; ribbon draws in 700 ms.
  • Sheets: critically damped spring (stiffness 320, damping ratio 1.0).
  • Hero figure rolls digit by digit, 420 ms.
  • Reduced motion: values swap, fades 120 ms.

Signature moment

The cash-flow ribbon on Home: income arrives from the left and splits into set aside, spent and free, echoing the logo's arrows. Tap a band to open it.

Fits because

Matches the calm, accurate apps users rate highest (Monarch, Copilot) and the patterns for a single honest number. Risk: can read as reserved; personality lives in the assistant.

Bahi

Warm, Indian and tactile, after the bahi-khata: the red-bound ledger shopkeepers still keep, with ruled columns, a red margin line and a stamp when an entry is done. Indigo ink, marigold highlights, buttons you can feel.

Type

₹72,932

आज ₹480 खाने पर खर्च हुए

Anek Latin and Anek Devanagari, by Ek Type (India). One variable family: condensed for big figures, wide for headings, and a Devanagari sibling for the Hinglish assistant.

Buttons and cards

Primary buttons have a 3 px pressable edge that sinks on tap. Ledger panels on khata paper with ruled rows; 14 px radius, no shadows.

Motion personality: springy and tactile

  • Presses: spring with slight overshoot (stiffness 420, damping ratio 0.72).
  • Save: the entry is stamped (scale 1.6 to 1, 220 ms) with a medium haptic.
  • Goal reached: a short marigold-petal burst, under 700 ms.
  • Reduced motion: stamp appears without movement.

Signature moment

The ledger itself: Activity reads like a khata page, day numbers in the margin, entries in the middle, the day's total on the right. Saving an expense stamps it.

Fits because

Original, India-native and warm where every competitor is a neutral western dashboard; tactile feedback borrows what users like in CRED without its slow flows. Risk: more decoration to keep disciplined.

Monsoon

Dark-first and data-forward, like a city street after rain: deep slate, luminous colours that mean something. The month is a living chart you can drag your finger across.

Light: F2F5F8 ground, ink 0E1620, data hues deepened (rain 1F6BCB, jade 0A7A57) to pass 4.5:1 on white.

Type

₹72,932

Bricolage Grotesque for figures and headings (optical size and width axes give big numbers character), Geist for interface text.

Buttons and cards

Pill buttons, pearl on slate. Cards 24 px radius separated by tone, not borders. Multi-hue data, one hue per meaning.

Motion personality: cinematic

  • Charts draw in 900 ms with 24 ms stagger.
  • Scrub: cursor follows the finger, a selection haptic per day, never faster than 60 ms.
  • Row to detail: shared-element morph, 320 ms.
  • Sheets: spring (stiffness 300, damping ratio 0.82).

Signature moment

The Pulse: Home opens on this month's cumulative spend against your plan and last month. Drag across it to read any day.

Fits because

Brings Copilot-level chart interaction to Android and India, where none of the polished trackers exist. Risk: dark-first is a strong taste; charts must stay simple for new users.

Mixes that work: Current's structure with Bahi's typeface and stamp-on-save; Bahi's ledger list with Monsoon's scrub-able chart on Insights; Monsoon's dark theme as the dark mode of Current.

Decisions needed before any code changes

  1. Which direction, or which mix?
  2. Bottom navigation: today the centre slot is the assistant and Add floats over content. All three mockups move Add into the centre slot and put the assistant on the FiNN mark in the top bar (long-press Add starts voice). Keep that, or keep today's layout?
  3. Add expense: amount-first keypad sheet with category, account and date up front and channel, payee and note under "More details". Every field stays; only the order and grouping change.
